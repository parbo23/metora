Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does Metora work?

Metora removes hidden metadata from photos and creates a cleaned copy.

2. Does Metora upload my photos?

No. Photo processing happens locally on your device. Metora does not upload your photos to a server for cleaning.

3. Does Metora modify my original photos?

No. Your original photo is left untouched. Metora creates a separate cleaned copy.

4. How do I restore my purchase?

Open Metora and use Restore Purchase in Settings or on the unlock screen.
